01 / ACM

PART OF A GLOBAL COMPUTING COMMUNITY

LEARN ABOUT ACM

The Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) is a global computing organization that brings together students, educators, researchers, and professionals across computing.

Our ACM Club brings that spirit into our university community through technical workshops, competitions, collaborative projects, and peer learning.

02 / WHY

WHY PROGRAMMING JAM?

Modern development is changing. AI can help developers plan, generate code, investigate bugs, and solve problems. JAM.26 gives students a place to learn how to work with these tools while developing the skills that matter: understanding problems, making decisions, debugging, adapting, and shipping something that works.

AI CAN WRITE THE CODE.

YOU STILL HAVE TO BUILD THE SOLUTION.

LEARN

Practical AI-assisted workflows and methodologies.

BUILD

Turn raw ideas into functioning applications.

SOLVE

Work through real architectural and logical problems.

ADAPT

Respond to changing requirements and unexpected bugs.

SHIP

Take something from abstract concept to production reality.
