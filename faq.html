04 Workshops

Yes. JAM.26 includes three workshop days on 15, 16, and 17 September 2026, all developed and taught by Shoug Alomran. Competition day is 19 September 2026.

Day 01 covers planning and the development workflow. Day 02 covers full-stack development and debugging. Day 03 covers deployment, domains, search discovery, and performance. Together they walk through the same workflow used on competition day.

Workshop attendance is strongly recommended because the competition assumes familiarity with the full workflow. Participants remain responsible for learning the published presentation material and completing the handout checklists before competition day.

All three workshop days, including what each one covers, are on the Workshops page.

06 Submission

Each team submits:

  1. Live production website
  2. Custom domain or subdomain
  3. GitHub repository
  4. Excalidraw functional diagram
  5. Working Firebase authentication
  6. Working persistent data functionality
  7. Required application features
  8. Completed unexpected change request
  9. Google Search Console setup
  10. Bing Webmaster Tools setup
  11. PageSpeed Insights result

The application must be functional during judging.

Yes. The final application must be publicly accessible for judging, deployed and connected to a domain or subdomain.

No. The judged submission may not be modified after the official deadline unless organizers explicitly authorize it.

Projects are scored out of 100 points across seven categories, covering functional completeness, technical implementation, problem solving and adaptability, UI/UX, system understanding, production readiness, and the final demonstration. The full breakdown is on the Competition page.
